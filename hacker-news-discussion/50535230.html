2026-11-17 · comment — If you have the quota or budget for it, I recommend vibe-coding your own personal benchmark. I have a suite of 8 projects that I made with models this year, and replay them - give the new model a checkout of the repo at that point, and the project spec. For the design task, I have it throw up a blind voting page for me to quickly judge, and for implementation I have a review agent do a general score and also hunt for problems/edge cases that I really found.
