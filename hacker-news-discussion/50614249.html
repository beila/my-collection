2026-11-23 · comment
Many improvements since a few months ago, many sites that were broken, now work just fine.

The settings screen is still very simple, but many good things already, like dark mode both for the browser as for the content as forced dark mode. Also they already provide native support for tabs on the left.

No extension support as yet, but they have a small native adblocking, where you pick and activate blocking lists.

The developer tools meanwhile is external, when you hit F12, it listens at :6000, then you have to open a gecko browser (firefox, librewolf, etcetera) and browse there to about:debugging, connect to localhost:6000 and then you'll have your devtools. At least dom inspector, network tab worked, debugger showed sources.

They already have WebDriver support, it at least responds, didn't further test this.

(It is not shown on the settings, but you can private browse by hitting ctrl+shift+n.)
