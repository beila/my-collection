2026-11-16 · comment — Hi! I'm Phillip Compeau, I teach in Carnegie Mellon's Griffin School of Computer Science, and I run CS Learn, which launched today.

We're releasing full semesters of lectures from current CMU computer science courses across a variety of topics, with one course premiering each day this week. More courses come in January.

Yes, MIT got here 25 years ago :). What I'm excited about is how we're doing it: a few of our students built tools that automate a lot of the production work, so a small team can get a course out quickly and still have people review every lecture.

We'll have lots more coming across a wide array of subjects. I'd love to know if there are topics you would like to see us cover!
