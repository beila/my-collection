Grammy Nominations 2027: See the Full List (Gift Article)

The artists, albums and songs up for honors at the 69th annual ceremony were announced Monday. The show will be held on Feb. 7 in Los Angeles and air on ABC and Disney+ after decades on CBS.

Raye is the top nominee for the 69th annual Grammy Awards, with 11, tying a record set by Beyoncé at the 2025 ceremony for most nominations for a female artist. The “Where Is My Husband!” singer will contend for record, song and album of the year, in addition to songwriter of the year, non-classical, and competitions across pop categories for the song and for her album, “This Music May Contain Hope.”

The British singer-songwriter, born Rachel Keen, joins Rosalía (nine); Ella Langley and Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo, Phoebe Bridgers (who each have seven); and Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas and Olivia Dean (six) as the top nominees.

In an announcement Monday morning, the Recording Academy revealed nominees for the 2027 Grammy Awards in 100 categories, the largest field since 2011. New among them are five awards: R&B collaboration or duo/group performance, traditional pop vocal performance, traditional folk album, best Latin song, and best Asian pop music performance. The K-pop supergroup BTS said in July that it would not submit “Arirang,” its latest album, in an apparent protest of the Asian pop category. “We hope music can be heard and loved for what it is, rather than being divided into region or language,” each member said in statements posted to their social media accounts.

Albums and songs released from Aug. 31, 2025, to Aug. 28, 2026, are eligible; the winners will be determined by nearly 16,000 voting members of the Recording Academy, among them performers, producers, engineers and songwriters.

The ceremony, which is scheduled for Feb. 7, 2027, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, will be broadcast on ABC and stream live on Hulu and Disney+ as part of a new deal between the Recording Academy and Disney. Previously, CBS had aired the ceremony for more than 50 years.
