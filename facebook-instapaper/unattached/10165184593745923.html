👀 🚃 Ever seen a train board a ferry? 🛳 This is China's first cross-sea railway ferry — the Guangdong-Hainan Railway Ferry across the Qiongzhou Strait in Hainan province. Because there is no cross-sea bridge or undersea tunnel across the Qiongzhou Strait, trains and vehicles cross the strait by ferry. Once a train arrives at the terminal, it is divided into four sections and pulled onto the ferry by a locomotive. Four railway tracks run along the bottom of the ferry's deck. When the ferry docks, the tracks on board are precisely aligned with those on land, allowing the train carriages to roll off the ferry and onto the terminal one by one.
