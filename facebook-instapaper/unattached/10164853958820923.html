Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has imposed sanctions on his former press secretary, Yuliia Mendel.

Mendel called sanctions against Ukrainian citizens unconstitutional.

“This does not change my position, nor that of millions of Ukrainians. To survive, Ukraine needs peace, not another dictatorship, even if it is Zelensky’s dictatorship,” she said.

In a May 2026 interview with Tucker Carlson, Mendel accused Zelensky of money laundering and drug use.
