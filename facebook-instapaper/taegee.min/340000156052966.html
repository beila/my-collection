Taegee Min: I heard this song at 2nd BESTOHA festival (Tokyo, 2004) for the first time.

Taegee Min

2012-03-11T14:28:00+00:00

I heard this song at 2nd BESTOHA festival (Tokyo, 2004) for the first time. At that time, Peking University from China (BE), SNU from Korea (SE), Tokyo University from Japan (TO) and Hanoi University from Viet Nam (HA) sang this together for their joint performance. I did not understand its meaning exactly at that time, but I could feel its motive definitely based on hope of future generation of east asia, and it was really touching. Today is one year to the Great Tohoku Earthquake. Hope everything is going well with my friends in Japan, and this song will deliver my message to them as I have got at that time.
