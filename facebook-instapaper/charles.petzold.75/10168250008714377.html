It is the power of Luigi Cerubini's opera "Medea" that the final scene is devastating and yet, we've come to understand why Medea.was driven to do what she did.

Composed in a somewhat fallow period of opera between Mozart and Rossini, "Medea" is rarely performed and Cherubino nearly forgotten, despite the fact that Beethoven considered him the greatest composer of his time.

So it's been wonderful seeing David McVicar's fascinating production (utilizing a large mirror angled down on the stage) both four years ago with Sondra Radvanovsky in the lead and this evening with Sonya Yoncheva.
