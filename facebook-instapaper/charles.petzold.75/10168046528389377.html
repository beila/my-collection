There are some people who think that Verdi's opera of "Macbeth" is superior to Shakespeare's play, and with a good production (such as the one we saw this evening by Louisa Proske) and great singers and actors (baritone Quinn Kelsey and soprano Lise Davidson in this case), it can be a persuasive argument.

Some non-musical highlights in this production: the stylized swordfight choreography with metal swords that loudly klanged with each strike, and the chorus of witches, who seemed to be observing every scene from the periphery and who delighted in the follies of the mere mortals living their lives full of sound and fury, signifying quite a lot about power and violence.
