Charles Petzold: The best part of a composer's significant birthday is the music!

Charles Petzold

Posted on 2026-10-14 20:37 UTC

Today we went to the first of four concerts that we'll be seeing this month celebrating the 90th birthday of Steve Reich.

This afternoon was a totally exhilarating concert with the great Japanese percussionist Kuniko Kato (in black in the first photo) with four other percussionists exploring Reich's percussion music spanning 42 years from 1967 to 2009: Music for Pieces of Wood, Nagoya Marimbas, Piano Phase arranged for two vinraphones, Part 1 of Drumming, and Mallet Quartet.

During curtain calls at the end, Kuniko Kato bolted up the aisle to embrace the composer, who has been sitting in the back row.
