Charles Petzold: "Drumming" is my favorite Steve Reich composition to see live.

Charles Petzold

Posted on 2026-10-29 01:26 UTC

"Drumming" is my favorite Steve Reich composition to see live. The ritualistic aspects -- the musicians huddling around the instruments banging out interlocking patterns; the moving around among the instruments over the 70-minute duration of the piece -- are immediately compelling as the listener is drawn deep into the music. Yet by the end it's no longer a ritual but an ecstatic celebration of communal music-making.

This evening we saw a spectacular performance of "Drumming" by the Sō Percussion quartet augmented by eight other musicians, and for an encore, they lined up six against six and performed a group rendition of "Clapping Music."
