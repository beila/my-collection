We have tickets for "Lincoln in the Bardo" on the 29th, but Deirdre was out of town and I realized today was opening night, so on the spur of the moment I got what appeared online as the last remaining orchestra seat: in.Row AA, which is one row behind Row Z and five rows from the standing room in back.

Still, I was able to enjoy the debut of an extraordinary opera, an opera that takes place entirely in a cemetery at night, in which all the characters except one are dead. This is a daring and successful and moving realization of George Saunders' novel by composer Missy Mazzoli (in the dark gown and brunette bangs in the curtain call lineup, standing between Peter Mattei as Lincoln and conductor Yannick Nézet-Séguin).
