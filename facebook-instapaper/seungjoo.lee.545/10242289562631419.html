A U.S. Navy sailor, Joshua Aviles, serving aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln, says his father, Luis Manuel Aviles Roa, was detained by U.S. immigration authorities while he was deployed in the Middle East.

Joshua said he had spent more than nine months at sea serving the U.S. and was devastated to learn his father could face deportation.

DHS said Luis Manuel Aviles Roa entered the U.S. illegally and would remain in ICE detention pending removal proceedings.

“Having a family member in the military is not a free pass to violate our nation’s laws,” DHS said.
