Russia is running out of cash to sustain its wartime spending, forcing the government into austerity after an April liquidity crisis left the federal Treasury with a negative 5.5 trillion-ruble ($65.3 billion) balance.

Since then, Moscow has cut non-war spending by 35%, ordered agencies to prepare for 15% staff reductions and postponed non-essential expenditures.
