Posted on 2026-10-22 23:25 UTC

I heard this news more than a month ago… but I guess it’s not too late to share! 😊

I am deeply humbled and honored to receive the Outstanding Mentorship Award from the Organizational Communication Division at NCA!

As the Division has organized a special panel, I am posting the schedule below in case you’d like to join. I am excited about this opportunity to share my stories and celebrate with you.

As my journey in the US academia has been (and always will be) a politics of survival, I recognize that mentoring requires resistance and imagination to envision different possibilities. That said, I have been able to stay here thanks to my community of care - the folks who support one another in firm solidarity.

Among many others, I am particularly grateful to Dacheng Zhang and Liahnna Stanley, who are now assistant professors producing critical and impactful scholarship. They are my former mentees, but our relationships can only be described as mutual learning (and mutual aid). Their nomination letters made me cry. I am simply so blessed to have them in my life as my best friends and colleagues. 💕

I am also sincerely grateful to Sarah Jane Tracy, my forever mentor, friend, and colleague. Since I began my position at ASU, her support, care, advice, and friendship have been vital to my personal and professional life. She was also kind and generous enough to write a nomination letter. As she is a fantastic role model, her recognition alone already meant the world to me, regardless of the award outcome.

In short… This is a tiny little thank-you letter to all of my mentors and mentees! The award is for you all! ❤️
