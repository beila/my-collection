The start of 2023 has seen the junior girls triumphant in the Leigh Academies Trust 5 A Side football tournament held at The Leigh Academy Dartford. Our squad of 8 remained unbeaten in their divisional games, beating Wilmington Academy, Leigh UTC, Stood Academy, Longfield Academy and Mascalls Academy. Scoring a total of 27 goals and only conceding 1 goal. The final saw a much closer match against Hundred of Hoo with SCWA winning 4-3. Humble in their win and showing excellent sportsmanship, the squad congratulated Hundred on Ho before the SCWA team captain went up to collect the cup on behalf of the squad.

Well done to Edie, Ava-Grace, Milly, Amelia, Jess, Rosie, Sophie, Darcie and all the LAT students that participated in the tournament.
